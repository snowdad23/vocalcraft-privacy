VocalCraft Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Developer: Snowdev Studios

Contact: prateekbehera1402@gmail.com

VocalCraft is an on-device vocal training app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: VocalCraft does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data. There are no accounts, and the app does not request internet access.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not send audio, pitch data, or practice history to any server, because the app has no network connection of its own.

Microphone and audio

VocalCraft asks for microphone permission so it can measure your pitch and give live feedback. Audio is processed on your device in real time. If you choose to record a practice take, the recording is saved in the app's private storage on your device. Recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded by us. You can delete them in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Data stored on your device

VocalCraft stores your practice history, scores, range measurements, lesson progress, and settings locally on your device. This data is not accessible to us.

If your device has Android's backup feature enabled, Android may include the app's local data in your own Google account backup, under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or access those backups.

Notifications

If you enable practice reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. It also requests permission to restart those reminders after the device reboots. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Paid features, if offered, are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google. We do not see or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

VocalCraft is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can revoke microphone or notification permission in Android settings at any time, delete recordings in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example by adding online features, we will update this policy and the effective date before the change ships.
